## 1. Working Capital Estimator

**Bundle:** Funding Readiness Widget Pack  
**Widget type:** Estimator  
**Primary CTA:** Apply Now  
**Partner use:** Funding pre-check for small business owners.

### Public Tool Concept
A working-capital estimator that helps business owners understand whether their revenue, deposit activity, cash position, and funding need suggest a realistic funding path.

### Embed Widget Plan
Inputs should include monthly revenue, average deposits, current cash balance, desired funding amount, time in business, industry, existing debt, funding urgency, and use of funds.

Outputs should include estimated working-capital range, readiness band, risk flags, documentation checklist, and next-step CTA.

### Flash-UI Asset Plan
Create a premium dark-luxe fintech landing page with a hero, estimator embed, readiness explanation, partner trust section, and funding CTA.

### Files To Create
- index.html
- embed.html
- styles.css
- script.js
- schema.json
- README.md
