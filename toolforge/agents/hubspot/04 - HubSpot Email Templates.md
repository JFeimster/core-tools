## 1. Purpose

This document provides reusable email templates for Moonshine Capital applicant, partner, and funding follow-up workflows.

These templates are designed for use across:

- Wix/site-side acknowledgment emails
- Gmail drafts
- HubSpot one-to-one emails
- Manual applicant follow-up
- Giggle / BankBreezy routing
- Business funding alternative lane follow-up
- Site membership and group access support
- No-response follow-up sequences

The goal is to keep outreach clear, compliant, human, and operationally useful.

Use these templates as starting points. Customize details before sending when the applicant’s situation requires it.

---

## 2. Core Email Rules

### 2.1 Draft First Unless Told Otherwise

When the user asks to “write,” “create,” “prepare,” or “draft” an email, create a draft.

Do not send unless the user explicitly says to send.

---

### 2.2 Match the Sending Context

Different sending contexts require different tone.

| Sending Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Wix/site-side acknowledgment | Operational, brand-safe, neutral, professional |
| HubSpot one-to-one email | Professional but more direct |
| Personal Gmail follow-up | Human, direct, strategic, Jason-style when requested |
| Partner email | Collaborative, confident, useful |
| Funding applicant nudge | Clear, motivating, action-oriented |
| Compliance-sensitive update | Careful, factual, no guarantees |

Do not use the same voice for every email. A website auto-reply should not sound like Jason just kicked the saloon doors open.

---

### 2.3 Never Guarantee Funding Outcomes

Do not promise:

- Approval
- Specific funding amount
- Specific terms
- Same-day funding as a guaranteed result
- Next-day funding as a guaranteed result
- Credit score improvement
- Underwriting decisions
- Final lender outcomes

Acceptable language:

- “You may be able to review options”
- “The next step is to complete the funding review”
- “This helps determine what may be available”
- “Based on what you shared, this appears worth checking”
- “Funding speed depends on the provider, completed application, bank connection, and underwriting review”

Not acceptable:

- “You are approved”
- “You qualify”
- “You will get funded today”
- “This guarantees same-day funding”
- “We can definitely get you the amount requested”

---

## 3. Tone Guidance

### 3.1 Brand-Safe / Wix System Tone

Use this for automated or semi-automated website messages.

Characteristics:

- Clear
- Professional
- Operational
- Helpful
- Neutral
- Low hype
- No slang
- No personal over-sharing
- No “Jason-style” punch unless requested

Best for:

- Application received
- Form confirmation
- Course/group access
- Membership acknowledgment
- General next-step confirmation

---

### 3.2 Personal Gmail / Jason-Style Tone

Use this when Jason is personally following up with an applicant or partner.

Characteristics:

- Direct
- Human
- Warm
- Strategic
- Slightly punchy
- Motivational
- Clear CTA
- Respectful urgency
- No fake corporate perfume

Best for:

- Funding applicant follow-up
- Giggle / BankBreezy next step
- Delayed applicant nudges
- Partner profile feedback
- Personalized funding strategy emails

This tone can be more conversational, but still must avoid guarantees.

# 20. Operational Standard

Every email should do one of three things:

1. Confirm receipt.
2. Move the applicant to the next action.
3. Clarify what is missing or pending.

If an email does none of those, it is probably just decorative smoke.

Moonshine Capital emails should be useful, direct, and trust-building. The applicant should know exactly what to do next without needing a treasure map, a lender decoder ring, or six follow-up emails from the abyss.
