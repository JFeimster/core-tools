# Funding Tracker Columns

Use these columns as a starting point for a funding tracker sheet:

- Lead Name
- Funding Goal
- Source
- Stage
- Owner
- Contact Method
- Due Date
- Last Touch
- Reminder Status
- Risk Notes
- Next Step
- Internal Notes

## Notes

- Keep the list short if the team needs speed.
- Add only the columns that support a real follow-up decision.
