# Skill Name

Lender Match Brief

## Purpose

Match a deal profile to the lender conversation that is most likely to fit it.

## Ideal User

Funding brokers and referral partners who need a quick lender-fit summary.

## Triggering Use Cases

- Lender routing
- Deal-fit screening
- Submission brief
- Referral handoff

## Required Inputs

- Deal type
- Borrower profile
- Funding amount
- Target timeline

## Optional Inputs

- State or region
- Risk flags
- Preferred product type

## Process

1. Summarize the deal in plain language.
2. Identify the strongest lender-fit signals.
3. Call out the main friction points.
4. Draft the handoff note or referral brief.
5. Keep the recommendation cautious and specific.

## Output Format

- Deal summary
- Fit signals
- Risk notes
- Lender handoff brief

## Quality Bar

Useful enough to send to a partner without extra rewriting.

## Guardrails

- Do not guarantee a match
- Do not promise pricing
- Do not overstate qualification strength

## Compliance / Risk Notes

- Keep the output as routing guidance, not a commitment.
- If the deal is sensitive, ask for a human review before sending.

## Example Prompt

> Build a lender match brief for this deal and call out the main fit signals and risk flags.
