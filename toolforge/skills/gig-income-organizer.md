# Skill Name

Gig Income Organizer

## Purpose

Organize mixed gig income into a simple view that supports funding readiness and cash-flow planning.

## Ideal User

Gig workers, founders with side income, and brokers helping non-W2 applicants.

## Triggering Use Cases

- Gig income summary
- Platform payout tracking
- Irregular income review
- Funding-readiness prep

## Required Inputs

- Income sources
- Payout cadence
- Expense categories
- Time range

## Optional Inputs

- Platform fees
- Tax notes
- Cash-flow gaps

## Process

1. Group income by platform or client.
2. Note payout timing and fee patterns.
3. Separate recurring income from one-off work.
4. Summarize the cash-flow picture.
5. Turn the result into a readiness-friendly brief.

## Output Format

- Income summary
- Payout pattern notes
- Cash-flow notes
- Ready-to-share brief

## Quality Bar

Easy to read and useful for a lender or partner who needs the full picture fast.

## Guardrails

- Do not overstate income stability
- Do not blur gross and net numbers
- Do not skip timing risk

## Compliance / Risk Notes

- Treat estimates as planning inputs, not proof of income.
- Keep tax treatment separate from workflow summaries.

## Example Prompt

> Organize this gig income into a clean summary I can use for a funding conversation.
