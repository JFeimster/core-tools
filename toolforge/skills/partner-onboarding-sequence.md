# Skill Name

Partner Onboarding Sequence

## Purpose

Create a simple onboarding sequence that gets a new partner to their first useful action.

## Ideal User

Affiliate managers, broker teams, and operator-led partner programs.

## Triggering Use Cases

- Partner welcome sequence
- Referral onboarding flow
- First-action checklist
- Partner enablement sequence

## Required Inputs

- Partner type
- Offer
- First action
- Compliance constraints

## Optional Inputs

- Asset list
- Tracking needs
- Follow-up cadence

## Process

1. State the partner role and goal.
2. Define the first action and the assets needed.
3. Sequence the onboarding steps in order.
4. Add disclosure and tracking notes.
5. Keep the handoff easy to execute.

## Output Format

- Onboarding sequence
- Asset checklist
- Follow-up notes
- Compliance notes

## Quality Bar

Short, useful, and easy to run without a custom ops system.

## Guardrails

- Do not overpromise earnings
- Keep disclosures visible
- Do not bury the first action

## Compliance / Risk Notes

- Preserve any required partner disclosures.
- Keep commission and routing language clear.

## Example Prompt

> Create a partner onboarding sequence that gets a new referral partner to their first action fast.
