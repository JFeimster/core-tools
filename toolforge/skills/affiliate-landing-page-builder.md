# Skill Name

Affiliate Landing Page Builder

## Purpose

Turn a partner offer into a landing-page brief that supports clicks, tracking, and conversion.

## Ideal User

Affiliate marketers, creators, and operators building partner-facing funnels.

## Triggering Use Cases

- Affiliate page brief
- Offer page outline
- CTA routing plan
- Tracking-friendly landing draft

## Required Inputs

- Offer
- Audience
- CTA destination
- Primary proof point

## Optional Inputs

- Tracking requirements
- Compliance notes
- Creative angle

## Process

1. Define the audience and the action.
2. Choose one core promise.
3. Map the page sections and proof points.
4. Add tracking and disclosure notes.
5. Keep the copy tight and reviewable.

## Output Format

- Page brief
- Section outline
- CTA plan
- Tracking notes

## Quality Bar

Clear enough to hand to a builder or copywriter without extra cleanup.

## Guardrails

- Do not hide affiliate disclosures
- Do not exaggerate outcomes
- Do not overload the page with mixed offers

## Compliance / Risk Notes

- Keep required disclosures visible.
- Preserve partner terms and tracking expectations.

## Example Prompt

> Build an affiliate landing page brief for this offer with one clear CTA and visible disclosure guidance.
