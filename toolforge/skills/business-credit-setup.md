# Skill Name

Business Credit Setup

## Purpose

Map the steps, assets, and checkpoints needed to start a business-credit workflow safely.

## Ideal User

Funding brokers, consultants, and founders building credit-readiness systems.

## Triggering Use Cases

- Business credit setup
- Credit profile checklist
- Vendor and routing prep
- Readiness education content

## Required Inputs

- Current credit stage
- Business entity status
- Target outcome
- Constraints

## Optional Inputs

- Existing accounts
- Timeline
- Compliance concerns

## Process

1. Identify the current stage.
2. Map the next practical step.
3. Separate setup items from growth items.
4. Call out missing documents or account types.
5. Keep the plan partner-safe and realistic.

## Output Format

- Step list
- Setup checklist
- Risk notes
- Next-step CTA

## Quality Bar

Sequenced clearly enough that a founder can act on it without guessing.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise credit outcomes
- Do not oversimplify underwriting
- Do not collapse legal and financial steps together

## Compliance / Risk Notes

- Keep the guidance educational, not legal or credit advice.
- Separate entity setup from credit-building claims.

## Example Prompt

> Turn this business-credit process into a practical setup plan I can publish in Core Tools.
